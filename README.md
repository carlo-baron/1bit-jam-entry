# ⚫⚪ Finger Hazard

![Gameplay Screenshot](screenshot.png)

> A fast-paced **1-bit shooter** made for a game jam — fight enemies, earn points, and blast your way through collapsing walls to uncover the tower behind them.

---

## 🎮 Description

**Finger Hazard** is a minimalist black-and-white shooter made for the **1-Bit Game Jam**.
Armed with a simple gun, you must defeat waves of polygonal enemies — each with unique speed and health — to earn points. Those points let you **break the wall** blocking the mysterious tower in the background.

Destroy enough of it, and you’ll reveal the entire structure — with **turrets that join your fight** as a reward for your persistence.

Every shot counts — and so do your fingers.

---

## 💡 Motivation

This was my **first solo game jam project** — built with Unity under time pressure, with limited experience but full dedication.
I focused on simplicity, style, and mechanical feedback instead of complexity.
It’s called *Finger Hazard* because you only shoot **one bullet per click**, so things can get… clicky.

---

## ⚡ Features

* 🕹️ **Simple 1-bit style** — pure black and white, no distractions
* 💥 **Enemy variety** — polygons with distinct health and speed
* 🧱 **Physics-based wall system** — blocks fall as you destroy them
* 🏰 **Interactive environment** — uncover a tower that fights back on your side
* 🔊 **Sound effects** for feedback and immersion
* 🧠 **Score system** — points fuel your destruction

## 🕹️ Usage

* **Left Click** → Shoot (1 bullet per click)
* **Right Click** → Break wall blocks using points
* **WASD** → Move
* **Defeat enemies** to earn points and clear the wall.
* Reveal the **tower** completely to win the game.
